# Venues

The Venues page keeps every place you are looking at in one list, so you can compare them and
find the details again when you need them. Once you have decided, choosing a venue for an
event puts its name and address on the invitations, on the guest's page, and in the calendar
entry guests can add from their invitation.

## Adding a venue

1. Open the wedding and choose Venues from the side menu.
2. Choose Add venue and fill in what you know. Everything but the name is optional:
   - The address. This is what guests see once the venue is chosen for an event, so write it
     the way you would on an envelope.
   - A website, email address and phone number for the venue itself.
   - How many guests it can take.
   - Prices: the hire price, a per-head price and the deposit, in the venue's currency, with
     a note for what is included or when payments are due.
   - Contacts: up to ten people, each with a role, email and phone, such as the events
     coordinator and the catering manager.
   - Notes for yourself.
3. Set the status: Considering, Shortlisted, Booked, or Ruled out. Booked venues are listed
   first.

Prices, contacts and notes are only ever seen by people in your account.

## Choosing the venue for an event

On a venue's card, choose Use for Wedding Day (or whichever event). Each event can have one
venue, and different events can use different venues. The choice is also on the event itself,
in Settings, where you can pick from your list or type a venue in by hand if it is not one you
tracked.

From then on, the invitation placeholders `{{venue_name}}` and `{{venue_address}}` fill in
from the venue, the guest's page shows it with a map link, and the Add to calendar links and
file carry the address. If you edit the venue's address later, everything follows; an
invitation email already sent keeps the wording it was sent with.

To change your mind, choose a different venue's Use for button, or choose the current one
again to stop using it.

## A link in the invitation email

The placeholder `{{calendar_link}}` puts a link in the email that adds the event, with the
venue address, straight to the guest's own calendar. New drafts include it; add it to older
ones from the Email tab if you want it there.

## Deleting a venue

Deleting a venue removes its prices, contacts and notes. Any event that used it keeps the
venue's name and address as typed-in text, so invitations are not left without a venue.
