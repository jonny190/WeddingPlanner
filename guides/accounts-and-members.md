# Accounts, members and passwords

## Inviting someone to your account

1. Open the account page from the menu at the top right.
2. Under Members, find Invite someone. Enter their email address and choose a role:
   - Viewer can look at everything but change nothing.
   - Editor can work on weddings, guests, invitations and seating.
   - Admin can also manage members.
   - Owner has full control. Only an owner can make someone else an owner.
3. Choose Send invitation. They get an email with a link that works for a week.

Until they open the link, they appear under Invited, not yet joined, and you can withdraw
the invitation from there. Sending another invitation to the same address replaces the first
link.

## Accepting an invitation

Open the link in the email. If you do not have a login yet, enter your name and choose a
password. If you already use Wedding Planner with that email address, sign in and you will
be brought straight back to accept. The invitation is tied to the address it was sent to, so
you cannot accept it while signed in as someone else.

Once you have joined, switch between your accounts from the menu at the top.

## Changing your password

On the account page, under Your login, enter your current password and the new one twice.
Your login is the same across every account you belong to, so the change applies everywhere.

## Forgotten your password

On the sign-in page choose Forgotten your password, enter your email and open the link in the
email we send. It works for an hour and only once. If it has expired, ask for another.

## Leaving or removing someone

Admins and owners can change a member's role or remove them from the Members list. Removing
someone takes away their access to this account's weddings; it does not delete their login.
An account always keeps at least one owner.
