# Menus and dish choices

If your caterer needs to know who is having what, set up a menu for the event. Guests are
then asked to choose when they accept, for themselves and for anyone they bring.

## Writing the menu

1. Open the wedding and choose Menus from the side menu.
2. Pick the event along the top. The day and the evening can have different menus, or only
   one of them might have a meal.
3. Choose Start with starter, main and dessert, or Add a course, and name each course.
4. Under each course, add the dishes guests can pick from. A note next to a dish is shown to
   guests too, so it is the place for "vegetarian" or "contains nuts".
5. Choose Save menu.

A course with a single dish is simply what everyone gets. Guests still see it, but have
nothing to choose. An event with no courses works as before: guests just reply yes or no.

Courses can be reordered with the arrows. If you remove a dish that guests have already
chosen, they are asked to choose again the next time they open their invitation, and the
page warns you before you save.

## What guests see

The letter mentions that there is a choice of dishes, and the menu sits on the reply slip
underneath. Once a guest ticks Joyfully accept they pick a dish for every course. If they are
bringing someone, a second set of choices appears for that person. They cannot send the reply
until every course is chosen, and the confirmation lists what they picked.

## Seeing the choices

Below the menu on the Menus page, Who is having what shows a count for every dish and a
table of everyone coming with their dishes and their guest's dishes. A guest who has not
chosen yet is marked Still to choose.

If someone tells you their choices by phone, choose Record next to their name and fill in the
courses you know. You can leave a course as not chosen yet.

Export for the caterer downloads a spreadsheet with one line per person attending: their
name, who they are with, their dietary requirements, and their dish for each course.

## Things to know

- Guests who decline, or are recorded as not coming, have their dishes cleared.
- Changing someone's reply to not coming on the Replies page clears their dishes as well.
- Dietary requirements are still collected separately; the caterer export shows both.
